Why it exists

Good open data is spread across dozens of portals, each with its own layout and access rules. Researchers and analysts can lose hours just finding the right dataset, and a lot of useful government and scientific data never gets found at all.

Open Data Gateway puts trusted sources in one directory, from the NASA Exoplanet Archive to UN climate indicators, WHO health data, and World Bank economic figures.

What's inside

A few of the sources in each category.

Science and research

NASA Exoplanet Archive, CERN Open Data, Allen Brain Atlas, and arXiv.

Government and policy

Data.gov (US), Open Government Canada, the EU Open Data Portal, and Argentina's Datos Abiertos.

Health

WHO Global Health Observatory, CDC BRFSS, Global Burden of Disease, and GHDx.

Economics and finance

World Bank, the IMF Global Debt Database, BIS statistics, and BLS data.

Climate and environment

Global Carbon Project, Global Forest Watch, Aqueduct Water Risk, and Carbon Monitor.

Transport

US BTS aviation and rail data, GTFS transit feeds, and bike share trip histories.

Who it's for

Anyone who needs reliable data and doesn't want to keep a folder of scattered bookmarks.

How it's kept: each database is checked and categorised by hand, with a direct link to the source. New portals are added as they appear.

  • Researchers and studentsFind clean datasets for papers and theses.
  • JournalistsCheck figures against the original source.
  • Startups and analystsFind data to build products and models on.
  • Curious peopleExplore what's openly available, from exoplanets to economic indicators.
