About me

I'm Jaed. I build websites, get businesses found on Google, and set up automations that take repetitive work off people's plates.

How I got here

My first job was in the public sector, running large data collection projects. That's where I learned how much easier decisions get when information is collected and organised properly.

I also have a degree in sociology, which taught me to pay attention to how people actually behave, not how we assume they will. I still use that every day: a website or an automation only works if it fits the way real customers and real teams do things.

From there I moved into freelancing, working with clients in different countries on content, marketing, and automation. Today most of my work is building websites that bring in enquiries, improving how businesses show up on Google and in AI search, and connecting the tools they already use so less gets done by hand.

How I work

  • One person, start to finish. You talk to me, not an account manager, so the website, search, and automation fit together.
  • People first. I design for the person filling in the form or reading the page, then automate what happens after.
  • Careful with data. I keep data collection to what's needed and can self-host automations so your information stays with you.
