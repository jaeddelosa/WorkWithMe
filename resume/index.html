Profile

I started in 2020 as a writer and editor, producing SEO articles for publishers like TechReport and PC.Game and B2B campaign content for Callbox. Writing for search taught me how people actually find things online, and that curiosity kept pulling me toward the technical side. I took over a full website and its search strategy at Lifeologie Counseling, administered CRM workflows and supported security monitoring at Sonitan, and along the way earned a full-stack developer certification and completed a network administration bootcamp.

Today I build conversion-focused websites, grow visibility on Google and in AI answer engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity (SEO and AEO), run Google Ads, and automate repetitive work with n8n, Claude Code, and MCP servers. The writing background never left: I still think first about what makes a person read, trust, and get in touch.
