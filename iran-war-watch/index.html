Why it exists

Coverage of the conflict is spread across dozens of outlets, and the oil market story is usually reported separately from the military one. It takes a lot of tabs to follow both.

Iran War Watch puts reporting from Reuters, BBC, AP, Al Jazeera, and others in one place, covering military developments, proxy conflicts, nuclear issues, and the risk of a wider regional war, alongside what it means for oil supply.

Why oil is part of it

Iran controls the eastern shore of the Strait of Hormuz, which carries about a fifth of the world's oil. Even a partial closure would hit prices in Asia, Europe, and the US.

The Oil and energy filter shows every story about crude prices, OPEC decisions, tanker movements, and pipeline security, from OilPrice.com, Reuters, and the business desks of major newsrooms.

Alerts

Stories sorted into three levels so the urgent ones stand out.

CriticalConfirmed military action or a major escalation, reported by more than one source.
WarningSharp moves in oil prices, threats to shipping, or new sanctions.
InfoDiplomatic contact, official statements, and background reporting.
