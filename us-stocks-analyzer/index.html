Why it exists

Most people who follow the market jump between several sites: one for charts, one for news, one for company figures, and another for the economic calendar. Real-time data often costs a subscription.

Stock Analyzer brings those into one screen, built on TradingView, with free real-time data for US markets during trading hours.

What it does

Enough for a quick check or a closer look.

Real-time quotes

Prices and key statistics that update during US market hours.

Saved watchlist

Your tickers stay between visits. Use plain tickers or the exchange format, like AAPL, BRK.B, or NYSE:JPM.

Full charts

TradingView charts with drawing tools, timeframes, and dozens of indicators.

Technical summary

A buy or sell reading based on common indicators, plus indicator overlays.

Company news

Recent news for each stock, so you can see what might be moving it.

S&P 500 heatmap

See which sectors are up or down at a glance.
