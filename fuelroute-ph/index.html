Why most fuel calculators get it wrong

Most calculators use a generic fuel economy figure and an old fuel price, so the number you get is a guess. Drivers end up either overbudgeting or being surprised at the pump.

FuelRoute PH uses efficiency figures for vehicles sold here and the latest local pump prices, so the estimate is closer to what you'll actually pay.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants to know what a drive will cost before they start it, from a weekend road trip to a week of deliveries.

How it works: Philippine vehicle efficiency data, pump prices updated from local sources, and route distances calculated for each trip.
